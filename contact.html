Please protect your personal information

Please do not email blood test reports, test values, medical records or other sensitive personal information. If you include a screenshot of a website problem, remove any names, results or identifying details first.

Email is separate from the checker: anything you send by email leaves your device and is handled by email services. See our Privacy Policy for information about the website.

Questions about your health or results

MyLabRanges provides general information. We cannot interpret individual blood test results, diagnose conditions or provide personal medical advice by email. Please discuss questions about your own health or results with an appropriate healthcare professional.
