Privacy

Privacy Policy

MyLabRanges is designed so the laboratory values you enter remain in your browser rather than being sent to us.

Blood test values you enter

MyLabRanges performs its result comparisons in your browser using JavaScript. The blood test values and reference ranges you enter into the checker are not uploaded to, or stored by, MyLabRanges on a server.

If you close or refresh the page, the site does not provide an account or server-side record from which those entered values can be retrieved.

Website analytics

MyLabRanges uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand basic site usage and performance, such as page views and page-loading performance. Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as privacy-first and states that it does not collect or use visitors’ personal data or track individual users across customers’ websites.

The analytics beacon does not read the blood test values entered into the MyLabRanges form.

Google advertising and cookies

MyLabRanges uses Google AdSense code and Google's consent-management service. The site has been submitted for AdSense review; ads may be displayed once approved and enabled.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalise ads using visits to this and other sites, subject to your choices and applicable consent requirements.

Advertising and consent services may process information such as your IP address, browser and device information, cookie identifiers, consent choices and interactions with ads. Consent services use cookies or local storage to remember your choices. The checker does not intentionally send your entered blood test values or reference ranges to advertising services.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your privacy and cookie choices

For eligible visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message offers Consent, Do not consent and Manage options. You can review the listed advertising partners and their policies, choose your preferences, and return to change or withdraw consent using the Privacy and cookie settings link in the footer.

If the link brings you to this section instead of reopening the message, the consent service may still be loading, unavailable, blocked by a browser extension, or not available for your location or while site approval is pending. Try again after the page has loaded. You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser settings.

You can manage Google's personalised advertising through My Ad Center, or opt out of participating companies' personalised advertising through YourAdChoices. Opting out of personalised advertising does not necessarily remove all ads or all service-related data processing.

Contacting us

If you email hello@mylabranges.com, your email address and anything you include are processed through email services so we can handle your enquiry. This is separate from the checker. Please do not send blood test reports, medical records or sensitive health information.

External links

The site links to external sources such as NHS information pages. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms. MyLabRanges is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site develops, particularly if new analytics, advertising or other third-party services are introduced.
